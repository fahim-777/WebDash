FAQ

Common questions.

What does the monthly care plan actually cover?

Hosting on UK servers, SSL certificate, daily backups, security patches, uptime monitoring, and up to 30 minutes of small content changes per month (text edits, image swaps, new opening hours, etc). It's £19, £29 or £49/mo depending on your plan.

Do I have to take the care plan?

No. You own your code outright. We can hand you the files and you can host it yourself or with another provider. The care plan exists because most clients want hosting and updates handled, not because we lock you in.

Are there any contracts?

None. The build is a one-off price. The care plan is rolling 30-day — cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

How fast is "live in 14 days"?

From the day we receive your content (copy, images, brand details). Delays usually come from waiting on those, not from us. We'll give you a clear content checklist on day one.

What's the money-back guarantee?

If you're not happy with your design within 14 days of launch, we'll refund the build fee in full. We've never had to, but it's there in writing.

Do you do bigger custom projects?

Yes — booking systems, customer portals, integrations, multi-site builds. Tell us about it and we'll quote.

Will my site rank on Google?

Speed, mobile-friendliness and proper schema all help — and they're built in. SEO is also about the content and links you have over time, which we can advise on. We don't guarantee rankings (no honest agency does).

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We can split the build cost into 2 or 3 monthly payments at no extra charge. Just ask on your call.
