About

Your technical partner — not your agency.

WebDash is a small UK studio. We build hand-coded websites for businesses who'd rather pay once, own the result, and never wrestle with a page builder again.

Hi, we're WebDash.

We started WebDash in 2022 because we kept rebuilding the same thing for friends running local businesses: a fast, simple website that didn't cost £8,000 from an agency or £40 a month from a SaaS that never quite fit.

We don't use Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Webflow or page builders. Every WebDash site is hand-coded — which sounds old-fashioned but is exactly why our sites load in under a second and never break when a plugin updates.

We pick up the phone. We answer email the same day. And if your site doesn't earn its keep, we'll work with you to fix it, not upsell you onto another plan.

How we work

Honest defaults.

Fixed prices, written down

Every quote is fixed before we start. If we underestimate, that's our problem — not a "discovery phase" invoice on top.

You own the code

We hand over the source files at launch. If we ever stop being a fit, you can take them anywhere. No lock-in.

Speed is non-negotiable

Every page is benchmarked on Lighthouse before launch. If it's not fast, it doesn't ship.

We say no to bad ideas

If a feature won't earn its keep, we'll tell you. You're paying us for an honest opinion, not a yes.
