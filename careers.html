Careers

Build websites you'd actually be proud of.

We're a small UK studio doing the unfashionable thing: hand-coding websites for real businesses. If you care about craft, speed and honest work, you'll fit right in.

Why WebDash

Small team. Real work. No nonsense.

We're not a 50-person agency, and we don't want to be. Here's what life at WebDash actually looks like.

4-day work week

Mon–Thu. Full pay. We get more done in 32 honest hours than most agencies do in 50.

Profit share

Every full-time team member gets a share of yearly profit, paid out in February. No vesting, no games.

Real ownership

You'll own projects end to end — design, code, client conversations. Not a Jira ticket factory.

£1,500 kit budget

Spec your own machine, monitor and chair. Renewed every three years.

Remote-first

Work from anywhere in the UK. We meet up in Manchester quarterly for two days of team time.

Learning budget

£500/year for courses, books, conferences — anything that makes you better at the craft.

28 days holiday

Plus bank holidays. Plus the week between Christmas and New Year, on us. Use it all — we mean it.

Pension & private health

5% employer pension match and Vitality health cover from day one.

No on-call

We build sites, not pagers. Evenings and weekends are yours.
