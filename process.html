Our process

From kickoff to live, in 14 days.

Most agencies take three months because their process is bloated. Here's exactly how we ship a bespoke website in two weeks — what we do each day, what we need from you, and where the time actually goes.

01

Day 0 · Before we start

Kickoff call & fixed quote

30-minute call to understand your business, who you're trying to reach, and what success looks like. By the end you have a fixed written quote and a delivery date — both on email, both binding.

  • You walk away knowing exactly what you'll get and what it costs
  • We send a content checklist (logo files, photos, opening hours, etc.)
  • 50% deposit invoice — work starts when it's paid
02

Day 1 · Monday

Sitemap & structure

We map out every page, every section, every call-to-action. You see the skeleton before we touch a pixel — much cheaper to change a sitemap than a finished design.

  • Annotated sitemap delivered by 17:00
  • 30-minute review call — sign-off or revisions
03

Day 2–3 · Tuesday–Wednesday

Design draft

Full Figma design of the homepage and one inner page template. Mobile-first — we design for the small screen and scale up. You get a clickable preview.

  • Two design directions to choose between
  • One round of revisions included on the chosen direction
  • Sign-off triggers the build phase
04

Day 4–9 · Thursday–Tuesday

Build

The longest phase, and the one you don't have to do anything during. We hand-code every page, integrate any forms / booking / commerce, and run our internal QA loop.

  • Daily progress link to a private staging URL
  • Lighthouse, accessibility, and cross-browser tested as we go
  • SEO basics wired in: schema markup, meta tags, OpenGraph, sitemap
05

Day 10 · Wednesday

Your review

We send you the staging link and a 5-minute video walk-through. You spend a day clicking around, on your own time, with your own devices. Send back a single list of every change.

  • Test on the phone you actually use
  • Show it to a colleague, your partner, your most pedantic customer
  • Compile feedback into one email — the more specific, the better
06

Day 11–12 · Thursday–Friday

Revisions & polish

We work through your feedback. Final Lighthouse run. Cross-device sweep. Backup of the existing site (if any) before we touch your domain.

  • Two revision rounds included in every plan
  • 301 redirects mapped from your old URLs to new ones
  • Analytics & Google Search Console set up
07

Day 13 · Sunday evening

Soft launch

We point your domain at the new site and watch the deploy. Most clients sleep through this — we like Sunday evenings because traffic is lowest if anything needs to be rolled back.

  • SSL certificate provisioned and verified
  • Old site backed up and archived
  • Post-launch monitoring active for the first 24 hours
08

Day 14 · Monday morning

Live & handover

Site is live. You get a 20-minute walk-through video showing how to update content, where to find analytics, and how to reach us. Final invoice (the remaining 50%) goes out.

  • Source code zipped & emailed — yours to keep
  • Care plan starts (hosting, SSL, backups, security patches included)
  • 14-day money-back guarantee window opens — read it on our guarantee page
∞

Day 14+ · Beyond launch

Ongoing care

Most clients stay on the rolling care plan. Hosting, security patches, backups, uptime monitoring, and up to 30 minutes of small content changes per month — all wrapped into one fixed monthly fee.

  • 30-day rolling — cancel anytime with 30 days' notice
  • You always own the source code, in any scenario
  • Monthly performance email so you can see your site's health at a glance

What if it slips? 14 days starts from when we have everything from you (logo, copy, photos, brand details). Delays are almost always content-related. We'll tell you upfront what we need, and if anything looks like it's pushing the timeline, we'll flag it on the call — not silently in week three.

Two weeks from now, your site could be live.
