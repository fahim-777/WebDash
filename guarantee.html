Money-back guarantee

14 days. Full refund. No drama.

We're confident enough in our work to put it in writing: if you don't love what we ship, you don't pay.

The promise. If, within 14 days of your site going live, you're not satisfied with the build for any reason, email us and we'll refund 100% of the build fee. No forms, no negotiation, no "discovery call" to talk you out of it.

What the guarantee covers

The full one-off build fee on every WebDash plan:

  • The Launch — £299 build fee refunded
  • The Growth — £599 build fee refunded
  • The Scale — £1,299 build fee refunded
  • Custom projects — full quoted build fee refunded

How to claim a refund

  1. Email hello@webdash.co.uk within 14 days of your site going live. One sentence is enough — you don't have to justify it.
  2. We'll confirm receipt within one working day.
  3. Your refund hits your bank account within 5 working days, by the same payment method you used to pay us.
  4. You keep the source code we delivered. It's yours either way.

What's not covered

To keep this fair both ways, the guarantee doesn't apply to:

  • Hosting & care plan fees. Months you've already had hosted aren't refundable, but you can cancel the rolling plan at any time with 30 days' notice.
  • Third-party costs we've paid on your behalf. Domain registration fees, paid stock photography, premium fonts, paid integrations — anything we passed through at cost rather than marked up.
  • Add-ons completed and delivered. Things like a separate brand identity package or photography day, which are billed as their own deliverables.
  • Refund requests after the 14-day window. After 14 days we'll still happily fix anything that's broken under our care plan, but the refund window has closed.

How we make this easy

We'd rather earn your business than trap you in it. To make refunds genuinely friction-free:

  • You always own your source code. We hand it over at launch and again on request — no separate fee for "exporting your data."
  • We don't tie payment milestones to your domain or hosting account. You can take both with you on day one.
  • We won't ask you to fill in a form, jump on a "save call," or wait 90 days. One email is the whole process.
  • If you'd prefer revisions over a refund, we'll do those instead — your call, not ours.

Why we offer this

Most agencies don't offer a refund because their work isn't easy to QA — it's locked in proprietary platforms, behind page builders, with monthly fees that pile up before you realise the build isn't great. Hand-coded sites are different: you can see exactly what we delivered, the file sizes, the load times, the design. The work is verifiable on day one.

If we can't ship a site you're proud of in 14 days from launch, we shouldn't be charging you for it. So we don't.
